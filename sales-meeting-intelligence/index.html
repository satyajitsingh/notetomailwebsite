Sales meetings

Know exactly where the conversation stands.

An opportunity is rarely one meeting. It's a discovery call, a technical discussion, a commercial conversation and a string of follow-ups. NoteToMail captures each one, pulls out the requirements, objections and commitments, and lets you see how they changed between meetings.

Positioning

More than an AI note taker. Less than an enterprise rollout.

Basic note takers give you a transcript and a summary and stop there. Large conversation-intelligence platforms give you far more — alongside a procurement cycle, an admin, and a deployment across the whole revenue organisation. NoteToMail sits deliberately in between: real meeting intelligence, meeting history you can search and question, and a view of how an opportunity has developed — for an individual seller, a consultant or a small team who can start using it this afternoon.
