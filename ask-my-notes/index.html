Ask My Notes

Ask your meetings instead of rereading them.

Type a question in plain language and get an answer drawn from your own meeting content. No scrolling through a transcript looking for the one sentence where somebody agreed to something.

Start for free → See example questions

Free plan included. No credit card required.

The problem

You don't need the transcript. You need one answer.

Most of the time you already know roughly what happened. What you actually want is a specific detail: the date that was agreed, the concern the client raised, the action nobody has closed. Ask My Notes lets you go straight to it — and the AI answers using only the content you provided, not assumptions about what a meeting like yours probably contained.

Three ways back into a meeting

Search when you know. Ask when you don't.

Keyword search, semantic search and Ask My Notes solve different problems, and they get better together as your meeting history grows.

🔍

Keyword search Pro

You remember the exact phrase, the person or the project name. Search finds the meetings that contain it.

🧭

Semantic search Pro

You remember the idea but not the words. Searching “budget discussion” finds meetings where finances were discussed even if that phrase was never used. More on meeting search.

💬

Ask My Notes

You don't want a list of meetings — you want the answer. Ask the question and read the response.

Example questions

The kind of thing people actually ask

Answers come from the meeting content you provided. The more detail your notes or transcript contain, the better the answer.

“What did we agree about the delivery date?”
“Which meetings mentioned the integration issue?”
“What concerns did the client raise?”
“What actions are still outstanding?”
“What were the risks mentioned?”
“What actions were assigned, and to whom?”
“What decisions were made?”
“Summarise the key points in three sentences.”
Two moments it earns its place

Before you send, and long after

✅

Check the AI understood you

Ask a question or two before you generate the follow-up email. If the answers match your memory of the meeting, the analysis has understood the content — and the email built from it will too.

🕒

Come back weeks later

Your saved meetings keep their transcript, analysis and generated email together. When someone asks what was agreed back in March, you have somewhere to ask.

Where the answers come from. Ask My Notes responds using the meeting content you supplied. It reports what your notes and transcripts contain — it doesn't add outside information or make judgements the conversation didn't support. Detailed notes give noticeably better answers than sparse ones.
