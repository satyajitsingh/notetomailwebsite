Interview meetings

Review interviews from the conversation, not your memory of it.

Capture an interview, get a structured record of what was discussed, and review it properly afterwards — including across several conversations for the same role.

Start for free → See meeting comparison

Free plan included. No credit card required.

Why it helps

Notes written during an interview are always the worst notes

Writing while listening means you miss half of what's said, and what you do capture is shaped by whatever you happened to be thinking at that moment. Capturing the conversation and reviewing the analysis afterwards gives you something closer to what was actually discussed — and something you can revisit when the interview is no longer fresh.

Per interview

What you get back

🎙

Capture

Record in the browser, upload an existing recording, or paste the notes you took. Online interviews can be brought in from Teams, Zoom and Meet on Pro plans.

📄

Structured review

A summary, the key points, and any decisions and actions that came out of the conversation — the next stage, the follow-up, who's doing what.

💬

Discussion themes

What the conversation actually covered, and how much of it. Useful when you realise a whole topic never came up.

✉

Follow-up

A professional follow-up email generated from the analysis, for candidates or for the hiring team.

Across a process

Several conversations, one role

An interview process usually runs across a first conversation, a technical stage and a final discussion. Saved meetings can be reviewed together rather than one at a time.

📊

Areas of strength

The topics where the conversation went into real depth, based on what was discussed rather than what you remember.

🔍

Gaps and open questions

What wasn't covered, and which questions are still worth asking at the next stage.

⚖

Comparison across stages

Compare related interview meetings to see how the discussion developed between stages. How comparison works.

An important boundary. NoteToMail does not make or recommend hiring decisions, score or rank candidates, or assess anyone's suitability for a role. It captures and analyses what was discussed so that the people running the process have an accurate record to work from. Every hiring decision, and responsibility for making it fairly and lawfully, remains entirely with you and your organisation.

Consent and data. Tell participants when a conversation is being recorded and make sure you have the permissions your jurisdiction requires. Meetings are encrypted and stored in compliance with UK GDPR, automatically archived and deleted after 12 months, and never used to train AI models.
