How it works

From a conversation to something you can use

Capture the meeting, understand it, find it again later, connect it to the meetings around it, see what changed — and then act on it.

01
🎙

Capture

Record a meeting, event or lecture in the browser, upload an existing audio, video or text file, or simply paste the notes you already took. On Pro, bring in Teams, Zoom and Google Meet calls. Works in 40+ languages.

02
🧠

Understand

AI turns the conversation into structured intelligence: a summary, the key points, the decisions made, the action items and their owners, the risks raised and what to do next — adapted to the kind of meeting it was.

Common questions

Notes and transcripts record what was said. Meeting intelligence goes further: NoteToMail analyses the conversation into a summary, key takeaways, decisions, action items and risks, saves it to a searchable history you can question, and lets you compare it with related meetings to see what changed. The follow-up email is generated from that intelligence rather than written from scratch. Read more about meeting intelligence.
Journey Intelligence helps you understand a series of related meetings as one evolving conversation. Bring meetings together into a Journey, compare them, and surface evidence-backed changes, strengths, differences and recurring themes across the meetings. See how it works.
Keyword search matches the exact words you type — useful when you remember a name or phrase. Semantic search matches meaning, so searching “budget discussion” will find meetings where finances were discussed even if those exact words were never used. Both are available on Pro plans.
