Recording a Meeting

Record directly in the app — no extra software needed

1

Click Start Recording

Grant microphone permission when prompted by your browser.

2

Speak clearly

Talk naturally. NoteToMail will capture everything said during the meeting.

3

Click Stop

When your meeting ends, stop the recording.

4

Wait for transcription

Your audio is automatically converted to text — usually within 10–60 seconds.

💡Long recordings take longer to process. A 1-hour meeting may take up to 2 minutes to transcribe.

Understanding the Output

What the Meeting Quality Score means

After AI analysis, you'll see a Meeting Quality Score out of 100. This gives you a quick sense of how much useful content was in your notes.

Score is calculated based on:

Number of action items
Decisions captured
Clarity of notes
Risks identified
💡A low score usually means your notes were short or lacked clear decisions and actions — not that the meeting was bad!

Common Issues

Quick fixes for the most frequent problems

❌ "No speech detected"

The recording may have been silent, or your microphone was muted. Check your microphone permissions in your browser settings and try again.

❌ Partial or incomplete transcript

This usually happens with very large files or poor audio quality. Try splitting long recordings into smaller parts, or re-record in a quieter environment.

❌ No insights generated

Your notes may be too short or lack clear actions and decisions. Try adding more detail — bullet points with named people, specific decisions and actions work best.

Best Practices

Get the most out of NoteToMail

🗣️
Speak clearly

When recording, speak at a normal pace and avoid talking over others.

🔇
Reduce background noise

Record in a quiet space for the most accurate transcription.

📋
Use bullet points

Structured notes with bullet points produce better AI analysis than long paragraphs.

✅
Include decisions & actions

Explicitly state who owns each action and what was decided for the best email output.

🎯
State key outcomes

End your notes with a short summary of what was agreed to help the AI draft a better closing.

🌍
Use your native language

Don't worry about writing in English — record or write in any language and NoteToMail handles the rest.
