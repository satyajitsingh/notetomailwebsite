A repository, not a folder of recordings

Your meeting history in one searchable place

On Pro plans, meetings are saved automatically once they've been processed. Open Meetings in the app and every session is there — the transcript, the AI analysis with its decisions, actions and risks, and the follow-up email that was generated from it. That's what search runs against, which is why searching finds meaning and not just filenames.

Two kinds of search

One finds words. The other finds ideas.

You'll use both, usually without thinking about which is which.

🔍

Keyword search

Matches the words you type. Best when you know something specific: a client name, a project code, a person, a phrase somebody definitely used.

Search: “Northfield migration” → the meetings where that exact term appears.

🧭

Semantic search

Matches the meaning behind the words. Best when you remember the topic but not the language people used to describe it.

Search: “budget discussion” → meetings where finances were discussed, even if nobody said “budget”.
