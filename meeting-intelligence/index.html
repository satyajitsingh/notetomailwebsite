2 — Understand

What the AI analysis gives you

NoteToMail adapts to the kind of content you gave it, so a project meeting and a training session are not analysed the same way.

🧠

For meetings and business notes

A summary of the conversation, the key takeaways, the decisions that were made, the action items and who owns them, the risks that were raised, and suggestions for what to do next.

📚

For lectures, training and research

The topic, a summary, the key points worth remembering and suggested follow-ups — because not every recording is a business meeting with owners and deadlines.

4 — Connect and compare

Meetings rarely happen on their own

A client relationship, a consulting engagement, a project or an interview process usually runs across several conversations. When related meetings sit together, you can look at the whole thread rather than one meeting at a time.

⚖

Compare meetings Pro

Select two or more saved meetings and view them side by side. Common themes, decisions and action items are surfaced across the set, so differences and repeats are easier to spot.
