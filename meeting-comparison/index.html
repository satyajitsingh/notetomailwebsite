What you're looking for

The differences that actually matter

Comparison is most useful when the meetings are related — the same client, the same project, the same role, the same recurring stakeholder call.

🔄

Progression

What moved forward between one meeting and the next, and what was simply restated in different words.

✅

Decisions

Which decisions were made, which were revisited, and which are still being discussed several meetings later.

📋

Actions

Action items that recur across meetings are usually action items nobody closed.

⚠

Recurring issues

A risk raised once is a note. The same risk raised in three consecutive meetings is a pattern.

🎯

Changing emphasis

Topics that dominated early and faded later — or arrived late and took over the conversation.

🔍

Evidence from the meetings

Every comparison sits on top of your saved transcripts and analyses, so you can open the underlying meeting whenever you want the detail.

When to use it

Good moments to compare

SituationWhat comparison helps with
Before the next client meetingRecall what was agreed last time and which questions you left unanswered.
Recurring project or stakeholder callsSpot the issue that keeps coming back week after week without being closed.
Across a sales opportunitySee how requirements and objections shifted between discovery and the commercial conversation. More on sales meetings.
Across an interview processReview several conversations about the same role consistently. More on interview analysis.
Picking up someone else's accountGet to the current state of a relationship without reading a year of notes.
Comparison and journeys

Comparison is how a journey becomes readable

Comparing two meetings answers a specific question. Following a journey answers a broader one: how has this relationship, project or opportunity actually developed? The two work together — comparison is the mechanism, and Journey Intelligence is the view it produces once your related meetings are grouped by client, company or engagement.

Journey IntelligenceUnderstand what changed across a whole series of meetings. Meeting SearchFind the meetings worth comparing in the first place. Meeting IntelligenceThe per-meeting analysis every comparison is built on.
